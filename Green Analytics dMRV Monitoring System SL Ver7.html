📄 Concept Note

Caribbean Gas Intelligence — St. Lucia Pilot · Full project rationale

1️⃣ Background & Rationale

OECS Trading proposes a new division — Caribbean Gas Intelligence (CGI) — to deploy a sensor-and-app platform that monitors LPG cylinder levels and biogas digester performance in real time, using the GCFT platform as its dMRV backbone. The pilot deploys in the Gros Islet/Rodney Bay corridor, St. Lucia's tourism heartland.

🔥 The Revolutionary Feature: When any monitored LPG cylinder drops to 5%, the system automatically sends an SMS or WhatsApp message to the user's preferred distributor — triggering an order without the user having to lift a finger. It also offers four payment methods — Wepay, bank cards, bank transfer, and pay-on-delivery.

📱 Wepay — The Recommended Method

Wepay is the Caribbean's most widely used mobile wallet. CGI is fully integrated with Wepay for seamless auto-payments.

Setup (One Time)

  1. Settings → Payment Methods → Add Method
  2. Select Wepay from the list
  3. Enter your Wepay mobile number
  4. Authorise via SMS — tap the link in the Wepay message
  5. Enter your Wepay PIN to link accounts
  6. Confirmation — the app shows "Wepay linked successfully"

How It Works

  • When the driver confirms delivery, the payment is auto-processed
  • You receive a Wepay SMS confirming the amount ($36 for 20 lb cylinder)
  • A receipt is emailed and visible in-app under Orders → Receipts
  • If Wepay balance is insufficient, we send a WhatsApp prompt to top up
💡 Why Wepay? No card required · Instant confirmation · No cash handling · Works on any smartphone · Refunds in under 24 hours. Transaction fees: $0.30–$0.50 per transaction (absorbed by CGI).

💡 No Smartphone? No problem. All alerts are sent via SMS and WhatsApp, which work on any mobile phone. You can reply to confirm refill orders without opening the app.

✍️ Step 2 — Create Your Account

  1. Open the app → tap "Create Account"
  2. Enter your mobile number — primary identity
  3. Verify via SMS — 6-digit code
  4. Set your PIN — 4 digits, secures account + payments
  5. Add your address — pin exact location on map
  6. Select preferred distributor — from CGI partner list
  7. Add backup contact — for emergency alerts

🔗 Step 3 — Pair Your Sensor

When the CGI technician installs your sensor, they give you a card with a pairing code. Here's what to do:

  1. Open the app → tap "Add Cylinder"
  2. Enter the 8-character pairing code
  3. Give your cylinder a nickname — e.g. "Kitchen"
  4. Confirm cylinder size — 20 lb or 100 lb
  5. Set alert threshold — default 5% (auto-order)
  6. Tap "Save" — sensor is paired and live

🛒 Step 6 — Ordering Refills

You can order any time — no need to wait for the 5% trigger:

  1. Tap "Order Now" on the home screen
  2. Choose your cylinder from paired list
  3. Pick a delivery window — same-day, tomorrow, or scheduled
  4. Confirm the price — shown with any fees
  5. Choose payment method — Wepay, Card, or Pay on Delivery
  6. Tap "Place Order" — SMS + WhatsApp confirmation
  7. Track your driver — live GPS in app

📦 Step 7 — What Happens at Delivery

  1. Driver confirms arrival (notification sent)
  2. Driver inspects empty cylinder
  3. Cylinder swap (2–3 minutes)
  4. Sensor re-attaches to new cylinder
  5. Payment processed — auto-charge or collected
  6. Signature or PIN confirms receipt
  7. You receive SMS: "Refill delivered. Rate your experience."
✅ Cylinder Live Within 60 seconds of the swap, your app will show the new cylinder at 100%. The next auto-order will trigger in ~4 weeks.

⚠️ Safety First Never install near an open flame or on a connected cylinder. Confirm valve closed and appliance disconnected.

🔧 Installation — Step by Step

  1. Confirm cylinder type & condition — 20 lb or 100 lb. Inspect bottom. Do NOT install on damaged cylinders.
  2. Position the cylinder upright on a flat, stable surface.
  3. Clean the bottom — cloth + alcohol wipe to remove dirt and oils.
  4. Activate the sensor — peel protective film; twist counterclockwise to unlock, clockwise to activate. Wait for green LED flash.
  5. Attach to cylinder bottom — center, press firmly. You should hear a click.
  6. Verify LED sequence — Blue (booting) → Green (ready) → Green pulse every 30 sec (transmitting).
  7. Record the pairing code in the CGI Technician App.
  8. Test reading — open app → "Read Sensor" → live reading within 30 sec.
  9. Hand over pairing card to customer and explain how to pair in their app.

✅ Activation Workflow

  1. Open the Technician App, log in with your credentials
  2. Tap "New Installation" — select the customer account
  3. Scan the sensor's QR code — links sensor to customer
  4. Verify sensor reading — matches expected fill (100%)
  5. Check LTE signal strength — 2+ bars required
  6. Force initial transmission — "Transmit Now" → confirmation within 30 sec
  7. Enter customer details + pairing code
  8. Take photos — sensor, cylinder, location
  9. Submit — auto-uploads to GCFT with confirmation ticket

  1. Review overnight auto-orders — first thing in the morning

🗺️ Route Optimisation

The AI plans optimal routes based on: customer locations · traffic patterns · driver availability · vehicle capacity · delivery windows · fuel costs.

📊 Typical Results 38% reduction in fuel · 30% faster deliveries · 65% less overtime · 95% truck fill rate

You can override any route. Drag-and-drop to reorder. Mark "must be first/last" for special cases.

💳 Handling Payments on Delivery

When a driver is dispatched with a "Pay on Delivery" order, they receive the amount due on their tablet. On arrival:

  • Driver swaps cylinder + attaches sensor
  • Driver presents options: cash, card reader, or Wepay on the spot
  • Customer chooses; driver processes payment
  • Receipt auto-generated (SMS + email)
  • Delivery marked complete in the system

💳 Daily Reconciliation Workflow

  1. Morning: Review overnight payments — check auto-charges, flag failures
  2. Mid-day: Process pending transactions — handle failed Wepay, verify card payments, confirm cash receipts from drivers
  3. Afternoon: Reconcile delivery data — match deliveries to payments (auto-match 95%)
  4. Handle exceptions — investigate unmatched transactions
  5. End of day: Generate reports — daily revenue, transactions by method, pending refunds

How to Process a Refund

  1. Find the transaction — by customer name, order #, or date
  2. Tap "Refund" — system shows original payment method
  3. Select refund type — full or partial
  4. Enter reason — dropdown of common reasons
  5. Confirm — refund initiated
  6. System auto-notifies customer via SMS + email

🧾 Generating Invoices

Commercial customers require formal invoices for each refill.

  1. Go to Accounting → Invoices → New
  2. Select customer account (marked "Commercial")
  3. Confirm delivery details — cylinder size, quantity, date
  4. System auto-populates price from contract
  5. Add special items (emergency, extended delivery)
  6. Generate invoice — sent via email + WhatsApp
  7. Record payment when received

💳 Payment Guide

Wepay, bank cards, bank transfers, and pay-on-delivery
Payment System NEW

📱 Method 1 — Wepay (Recommended)

Wepay is the Caribbean's most widely used mobile wallet. CGI is fully integrated with Wepay for seamless auto-payments.

Setup (One Time)

  1. Settings → Payment Methods → Add Method
  2. Select Wepay from list
  3. Enter your Wepay mobile number
  4. Authorise via SMS — tap link in Wepay message
  5. Enter your Wepay PIN to link accounts
  6. Confirmation — "Wepay linked successfully"

How You Pay

  • Driver confirms delivery → payment auto-processed
  • You receive Wepay SMS confirming amount ($36 for 20 lb)
  • Receipt emailed + visible in-app under Orders → Receipts
  • If balance insufficient → WhatsApp prompt to top up

💳 Method 2 — Bank Cards

Accepted Cards

  • Local debit — Bank of Saint Lucia, RBC, 1st National Bank, CIBC FirstCaribbean
  • International — Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • Prepaid — where supported by issuer

Setup

  1. Settings → Payment Methods → Add Method
  2. Select "Bank Card"
  3. Enter card number, expiry, CVV
  4. Verify with $1 charge (immediately refunded)
  5. Enter 3D-Secure code if prompted
  6. Card saved for future auto-charges
🔒 Security Card data is never stored on CGI servers. All processing via PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Only tokenised reference is stored.

💵 Method 4 — Pay on Delivery

No pre-payment, no card on file — pay the driver when your refill arrives.

Enabling Pay on Delivery

  1. Settings → Payment Methods
  2. Toggle "Pay on Delivery" ON
  3. Select which cylinders this applies to
  4. Optionally add max order value (e.g. under $400 XCD)
  5. Save

🏠 Customer FAQs

Do I need a smartphone to use CGI?
No. All critical alerts are sent via SMS and WhatsApp, which work on any mobile phone. However, the app provides richer analytics and self-service options.
Is the Mopeka sensor safe on my cylinder?
Yes, completely safe. It attaches magnetically to the OUTSIDE bottom of the cylinder. No drilling, no wiring, no contact with gas. Certified for use in LPG environments.
Can I disable auto-ordering?
Yes. Open the app → Settings → Auto-Order → toggle off. You'll still get alerts, but you'll need to manually order.
Can I use different distributors for different cylinders?
Yes. In Settings → Preferred Distributor, you can assign different suppliers to each cylinder paired to your account.
What happens if my Wepay balance is too low?
If a Wepay charge fails, the system automatically retries twice. If still failing, you'll get a WhatsApp prompt to top up. Meanwhile, the delivery goes ahead and you can pay cash on arrival.

🔧 Technician FAQs

What if the cylinder has a rusted bottom?
Use moisture-resistant epoxy on the clean surface before attaching the sensor. If the cylinder is structurally compromised, do NOT install — report to Operations for a replacement.
How long does the Mopeka sensor last?
5-year battery life with normal use. The sensor itself is expected to function 10+ years. Standard CR2032 battery.
Do I need to configure Wi-Fi?
No. The sensor uses cellular LTE Cat-M1 directly. No Wi-Fi setup needed. It works anywhere with LTE coverage.
What if the customer refuses installation?
Log the refusal in the Technician App ("Customer Refused"), take a photo of the cylinder or door, and contact your supervisor. Never install without documented consent.

🚚 Operations FAQs

Can I override the AI route suggestion?
Yes. Drag-and-drop the delivery sequence. Mark stops as "must be first" or "last". The AI re-optimises around your constraints.
What if a driver becomes unavailable mid-shift?
The system shows alternative drivers with estimated arrival times. One tap reassigns the order. Drivers are auto-notified.
How do I handle failed deliveries?
Mark as "Failed — Rescheduled" and select a new window. Customer receives SMS explaining the change and can respond to confirm.

💰 Admin FAQs

How long do refunds take?
Wepay refunds: under 24 hours. Card refunds: 3–5 business days depending on issuing bank. Refunds initiated before 3 PM typically credit same day.
How do I verify a disputed charge?
Check the blockchain ledger. Every transaction is logged with an immutable hash. From Accounting, tap "Verify" on any transaction to see the original record with timestamps, GPS, and sensor data.
Can I generate custom reports?
Yes. Accounting → Reports → select date range, filters, format (Excel or PDF). Reports include revenue, transactions, refunds, and reconciliation status.
